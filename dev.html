About the developer

Keepeetron, aka. keepee, is the developer for sobosuba. He has been making sobosuba for over 2 years and have been a developer for longer. They have made multiple games and other projects, but sobosuba is their first project that they have posted on the Steam store. Sobosuba was a passion project they made, and they expected it to not get much popular, but the Japanese community found the same and related it to Suika Game but with soft-bodies, and it got a bit of traction, though not much.

A popular Twitch streamer named DougDoug ended up streaming the game online to his 10,000+ viewers, and made the game much more popular, to the point where it got on to the Steam 250 weekly chart for game sales. He is still working on sobosuba, and is making more modes for updates and such.
