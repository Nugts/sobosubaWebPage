What's Sobosuba?

Sobosuba is a puzzle game available on Windows & Linux about shooting and merging soft-body blobs, called "sobos", with the goal of merging as many sobos as you can for bigger and bigger sobos and high-scores. The game uses strategy and skill as a forefront in how you play. You can strategize and envision where a sobo might end up at with a tricky spot, or plan a chain from a small sobo to create a huge sobo. The game also features achievements, which gives you points to unlock different color styles to make your game look cooler while playing. Some achievements you could get while playing normally, while other achievements might need you to go out of your way for them.
