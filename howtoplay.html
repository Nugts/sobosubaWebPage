How to play Sobosuba

To play Sobosuba, there are barely any controls, and it's a pretty easy game to learn. The controls you need to know are:

  • Left click and hold on a sobo
  • Drag to aim your sobo
  • Release left click to launch your sobo
  • Right click to cancel launch

You can also use a controller to play, using your thumbsticks and triggers to select and control the sobo.

To change settings, and style, see achievements, and other important things, you can use the menu, which you can access by clicking the sobosuba logo on the left. From here, you can do things like change style, see previous scores, start a new game, and more.
